# YouTube video on macOS

## Sub-features

- `video-browser` offers a personalized regular-YouTube **For you** feed for the selected account/channel, plus public free-text search, thumbnail cards and paginated results with retry. Account changes clear the feed and cancel pending home/search/pagination requests; stale responses cannot restore another profile's videos. Signed-out users see a sign-in invitation and can still search. There are no preset topics.
- `video-url` opens one YouTube video in a separate native window from the account menu. Short, watch, Shorts, live and embed links are accepted; arbitrary websites, files and playlist-only links are not.
- `video-handoff` pauses music when video starts or resumes. Music Play pauses video. A stream still resolving when video opens stays paused and is not recorded as heard. Closing video leaves music paused and Meiro open.
- `video-native` covers GPU presentation, VideoToolbox/CPU decoder reporting, pause, exact ±10-second seeks, volume, fullscreen, close/reopen and application shutdown.
- `video-captions` offers uploaded and automatic YouTube captions through a native CC menu. Only a chosen language is downloaded; captions reset to Off when the video changes.
- `video-history` adds started videos to the existing local history and stats. Loading, failure or cancellation alone does not count; pause/resume and seek do not duplicate a play.
- `video-progress` saves the last native video's position in playback.json independently of the music queue. A window left open at quit reopens paused; an explicitly closed window stays closed. Signed-in videos use authenticated WEB playback/watch-time sessions with podcast-style 20-second reporting and final intervals. Local positions win over the WEB player's suggested start position; signed-out and failed account requests fall back to local playback.

## How to get to it (user POV)

On macOS, install `mpv` and `yt-dlp` with Homebrew. Open YouTube in the navigation rail for the selected profile's For you feed, or type in Search YouTube videos and press Enter, then click a card. For you or clearing search returns to recommendations. More videos appends results; failed pagination keeps existing results and offers Retry more videos. Selecting another card replaces the video in the same window. Account → Open YouTube URL remains available for pasted links; while a video window exists that menu entry brings it forward. Account-restricted playback is not part of this feature.

In the video window, open CC: Off and choose a language. Automatic tracks are marked automatic; uploaded captions are listed first. Choose Off to hide subtitles. CC: Loading… and CC: Unavailable are disabled when the video is still loading or offers no captions. YouTube may restrict a caption download even when its language is listed; the player reports that failure without stopping the video.

Quit Meiro with a video open and relaunch: the same video loads at its saved position and remains paused until Play. Closing the video window first prevents it reopening automatically; choosing that same video again still resumes its local position. A nearly finished video starts over. When signed in, another video's account resume hint is used only when no local position is available. Search stays public; home recommendations and progress reporting use the selected account. Neither gives yt-dlp account credentials.

## Gotchas

- Run a real macOS window for `video-native`; headless tests cannot prove hardware decoding, audio/video synchronization or native view lifetime.
- Do not ask mpv's ytdl hook to import every automatic translation. Videos with many uploaded languages can expose thousands of translated variants and exhaust native threads. Request `sub-langs=-all`, read the extractor's subtitles/automatic_captions metadata, and use sub-add only on selection. Do not enable write-auto-subs solely to read metadata: it also expands translations of every uploaded track. The native menu can render above the embedded OpenGL view; an in-window popup cannot reliably do so.
- Exercise signed-out live search, pagination and switching between two cards in the same player. Thumbnail downloads may take longer than search; wait for images before capturing the catalogue. Failed playback may leave mpv's path on the previous video; show its error rather than waiting forever for the new path.
- Respect yt-dlp's `available_at` on resolved formats before opening streams: newer YouTube responses can require a short wait and return HTTP 403 if mpv opens them immediately. The post-extraction on_load hook waits off-main, with cancellation on close. Its user-data stdout property is a JSON-quoted string containing the extractor's JSON.
- MyGo automation captures omit the embedded OpenGL view. Use ScreenCaptureKit window captures for actual video frames; read the UI tree for controls and the decoder indicator. Refresh the capture before reading an occluded window's tree.
- The video window owns background render/poll workers. Closing cancels them, waits off the main thread and releases the retained native view on the main thread. App shutdown must also wait/release if its final UI callback cannot run. Exercise closing during loading and repeated reopen under the race detector.
- Real network tests stay out of `go test`. Use the built application with fresh app data and no real account. `videotoolbox (h264)` is decoder evidence; a nonblank native capture and advancing position establish presentation. This is not a signed/notarized packaging test: libmpv remains a Homebrew dependency.
